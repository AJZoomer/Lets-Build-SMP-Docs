What is Let's Build SMP?

Let's Build SMP is the official Minecraft community server for the Let's Build Discord. The server carries a strong BettaCraft SMP vibe and is heavily inspired by BettaCraft SMP.

Community Focus

The SMP is designed for builders, creators, and community-driven players who enjoy collaborative projects, themed seasons, and a friendly environment.
