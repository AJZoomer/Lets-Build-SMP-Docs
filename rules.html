Server Rules

  1. Follow All Discord Server Rules
    If it's not allowed in the Discord, it's not allowed on Let's Build SMP.
  2. No Stealing
    Do not take items from chests without explicit permission. If you pick up someone’s dropped items, return them immediately unless told otherwise.
  3. No Griefing
    Do not destroy, modify, or move other players' builds. This includes fire, TNT, or manual destruction. Griefing results in an instant permanent ban.
  4. No Hacking or Duping
    Hacked clients, X-Ray (including resource packs), aim-bots, autoclickers, glitch exploitation, and duping are prohibited. TNT dupers for farms are the only exception.
  5. No PvP Without Agreement
    You may only PvP players who have agreed to it. Traps inside bases are not allowed unless the player has been properly warned.
  6. No Offensive Skins
    Any non-PG or rule-breaking skin results in an instant permanent ban.
  7. Keep Lag Low
    Limit mob pens to 10 mobs or fewer. Only build villager halls you need. Turn off farms when leaving to prevent lag.
  8. Staff Have Final Say
    Staff decisions are final. Refusing to listen will result in a ban.

Reporting Rule Breakers

If you see someone breaking the rules, please create a ticket in the Discord.
