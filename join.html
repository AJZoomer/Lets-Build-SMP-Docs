How to Join

Joining the Let's Build SMP requires linking your Minecraft account through the Let's Build Discord server.

Step 1 — Reach Level 10

Be active in the Discord and reach Level 10 through chatting.

Step 2 — Attempt to Join the Server

Join using the IP: AJZoomer-Lets-Build.aternos.me

You will be disconnected and given a code.

Step 3 — Link Your Account

Send the code to @Let's Build Bot in Discord to link your account.

Step 4 — Access Granted

Once linked, you will be able to join the SMP normally.
